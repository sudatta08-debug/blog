The Republic of Singapore is a country located in Southeast Asia. It's an island nation that has one primary island and over 60 smaller islets. This territory is also known as one island. Despite being tiny, Singapore has plenty of things to do to keep busy. This city-state is known for its unique attractions and extremely well-maintained cleanliness. There are many different things to see and do in Singapore, and it's not hard to find something that's just for you.

Singapore has embraced many challenges and has reshaped itself as one of the most expensive countries in the world. It is a hub for finance professionals, including thousands of ex-pats and millions of tourists.

Over the years, Singapore has been gradually building its reputation as a city that is full of natural beauty combined with modern-day high-rise architecture. As the country continues to uproot its urban development, architects and urban planners have been working together to weave nature into the new developments. Besides planting trees and gardens, they have also been incorporating various other sustainable practices into the design of new buildings.

So if you are planning to visit Singapore, then this article will guide you to make a small itinerary for your trip

## 1\. The Stunning Architecture

Singapore's architecture is influenced by its various cultural and ethnic backgrounds. Its contemporary design seeks to be sustainable, and some examples of this can be found in high-rise buildings. Green ventilation and architecture are also important factors to consider when it comes to Singapore's humid climate.

From the former Supreme court to the stunning Helix bridge and from the world-famous Marina bay sands hotel to the Thian hock Keng temple, every building has its own rich history with breathtaking views.

## 2\. Eating At Hawker Centers, Drinking From Vending Machines

One of Singapore's best ways to eat cheap and clean food is to visit one of its numerous street food stalls. They are basically known as hawker centres, and people prefer eating in hawker centres for most of their meals. These are typically open-air structures that are used by the majority of the country's residents to gather quick and delicious meals.

The best options are Maxwell, Chinatown Complex Food Centre, and Lau Pa Sat. Apart from that, you can also experience dynamic food culture in Clarke Quay, where the nightlife is very vibrant and groovy.

Apart from eating, you can get really affordable and healthy juice options from the vending machines that are placed on the streets. The entire process of getting a refreshing and cold juice is easy and satisfying at the same time.

## 3\. Shopping

If you are a shopaholic, then Singapore is the place for you. It is a vibrant shopping destination that has hundreds of shopping centres and is known for its fashionable products. Many tourists come to this city-state to see the sights and get the best deals.

Despite the abundance of world-class brands, Singapore still offers its customers affordable yet fashionable products. This city-state does not limit itself to only offering high-quality products. Its shoppers can also find various items that suit their tastes and wallets.

The city of Singapore has over a hundred malls that cater to different types of shoppers. One of these is ION Orchard, which is a multi-level mall that features a variety of luxury brands such as Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and Prada. It also has over 300 high-street and lifestyle stores.

Bugis is one of the best street shopping experiences with many affordable options whereas the area has two massive malls that feature international brands, such as Gap and Marks & Spencer. Another great mall in Singapore is Raffles City, which has a wide selection of both regular and fancy stores.

## 4\. Changi

The Changi Airport is itself a destination when it comes to experiencing a holiday. The airport is equipped with so many fun activities that every age group can enjoy every bit of it.

The name of the airport terminal is known as the Jewel. With a wide variety of shopping options and attractions, it is a must-visit for both tourists and locals. The world's largest indoor waterfall, "The Jewel", is also located within the airport, and there are numerous suspended rides and shops that combine retail therapy and nature.

From a casual movie theatre to the butterfly garden, you name it, and Changi has it. So make sure you plan a full day only to spend at Changi International Airport.

## 5\. Explore The Man-made Natural Beauty

Did you know the former Prime Minister of Singapore, Lee Kuan Yew, is the man responsible for the exponential growth that we are witnessing today? It was his futuristic vision that we can see today as he has smartly blended the greenery, the modern architecture, and the appropriate transportation in the country.

Gardens By The Bay is a living example of how nature can survive beautifully just in the middle of the city. It is not only maintained by the constant hard workers but also has been taken care by the government personally to keep its beauty intact.

Singapore has very small land and no natural resources. Yet somehow, it is the richest country known to the global population. Singapore is a free-market success story, and conservatives believe that it is due to its liberal immigration policies. The country not only provides a high standard of living but, more importantly, a healthy one which is primarily because of the greenery every step of the way.

## 6\. Know About The History Of Singapore

No, we are not going to discuss the history of the country but rather where to visit if you are keen to know it. Singapore has many historical as well as modern museums where you can spend time to know more about its history.

One of them is the National Museum of Singapore which features cutting-edge galleries that exhibit the city-state's development through interactive and immersive exhibitions.

Apart from that, there is the National Gallery, the Art Science Museum, and the Asian Civilisations Museum, where you can culture a lot about Singapore’s ancient past.

## 7\. Sentosa Fun

The Sentosa Island is the definition of fun in Singapore. The Sentosa Resorts World is a multi-awarded resort that features a variety of world-class attractions. Some of these include the S.E.A. Aquarium, Universal Studios, and the Maritime Experiential Museum. It also has six unique hotels and an exclusive casino. The resort offers a superior dining experience and a variety of entertainment options.

Not only indoor experience, but people mostly go to Sentosa island to enjoy the beach with their families.

## 8\. Local Transport

In Singapore, public transport is a convenient and comfortable way to get around the city. Its high interconnectivity and cleanliness standards make it an ideal choice for people who live in the country. Also, due to the high cost of owning and maintaining a car, many people prefer to use public transport.

Getting around Singapore can be done using the city's Mass Rapid Transit system (MRT), which has lines that run across the entire city. However, if you're in a specific area, walking is the best option. The system also provides bus routes that can take you to different parts of the island.

Due to Singapore's size, it's not that difficult to explore the city on foot. You can still take advantage of the many attractions that the country has to offer by walking through its neighbourhoods. The Tourism Board has a variety of maps and walking tours that will help you navigate the city. And you can see people walking at every hour, which will compel you to walk as well.

## 9\. Adapting Healthy Lifestyle

Although it is important to take care of your body, many people neglect their own. This can lead to poor physical health and a lack of energy. Getting enough sleep and eating a healthy diet are some of the most important factors that can improve your health. Having a healthy and active lifestyle can help keep you feeling great and motivated.

You can notice people residing in Singapore adopt a great healthy lifestyle as they tend to run, cycle, and take long walks to keep themselves energetic. While South Asian food also contributes to that factor, you will find all kinds of different cuisines yet won’t have a guilty meal due to the constant exercise.

## 10\. Different Cultures - India, China, Middle East

Believing in the importance of multiculturalism, Singapore has established a framework for addressing the country's various cultural and racial differences. At the outset, it was decided that everyone should be treated equally. This has resulted in a relative racial harmony.

There is Little India that will completely give you the proper Indian vibe with temples, Indian food, and Indian music on every corner of the streets. The place is vibrant, with many hotels and shopping options which Indians can relate to.

Similarly, in Chinatown, there are hawker centres, Chinese temples, and rich Chinese culture that you can experience just by taking a walk down the lanes.

Not only South Asian, but you also get to experience the Middle Eastern culture in Singapore. If you crave a Turkish kebab or hot Arabic Shawarma, Arab Street is the right place to visit.
