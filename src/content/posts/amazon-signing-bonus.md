[Amazon](http://www.amazon.in/) is one of the biggest American multinational tech companies that have a wide range of businesses across the globe. The company is widely known for its e-commerce and retail services but, it also offers services like digital streaming, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence. As a huge tech giant, the company is additionally famous for its corporate environment.

Amazon, as an employer, offers several perks to its employees from time to time. One of its impressive gestures is the [signing bonus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signing_bonus) arrangement for new employees. Signing bonuses are one-time introductory payments made by the company to new hires. Amazon provides its new employees with signing bonuses, which can range from 1000 to 3000 dollars depending on their designations. Apart from the bonus, the company also allows access to its’ stocks to its employees. In order to help employees feel that they are a big part of the company, Amazon offers its employees several other benefits other than paying them fairly. During sales, holidays, and even the pandemic, business was intact, and workers worked a lot and were offered coupons for other benefits as a thanksgiving for their efforts.

When it comes to providing bonuses, Amazon as a company leads the list. It is not a secret that Amazon has been hiring crazy throughout the coronavirus pandemic (2020-21) and also sketching its physical expansion of warehouses and other facilities. Even though the company is hiring in huge numbers but that doesn’t mean getting a job in the company is easier. The company is hiring both technical and non-technical workers due to its broad expansion plans. The mission of Amazon is to serve its audience as effectively as possible.

_No Competition With Amazon_ – One of the main reasons for the attractive pay range is that Amazon targets talented applicants to always consider its company over any other. Due to the pandemic, the businesses of online retail stores have increased wildly. And Amazon had a really successful business growth due to consumers’ heightened interest in online shopping. Rather than just offering a handsome salary, Amazon focuses on the weak points of its competitors and provides the applicants a prominent figure which can be an offer hard to reject. As per recent news, Amazon is trying to recruit thousands of employees to handle the latest increased workloads. During the holiday season, workload elevates due to the rush in orders. That is why the company always gathers enough workforce to deal with busy situations.

_Amazon Doesn’t Lose Talent Because Of Salary –_ When it comes to the hiring process of the company, Amazon makes sure that it gets all the talents out there and the salary negotiations shouldn’t be an issue. Amazon pays attractive salaries to its employees so that they will be excited to work for the company for a long time. The company also aims to improve the safety measures of the employees and its work environment during the pandemic. The company also offers a small portion of the equity along with the signing bonus. Amazon always focuses on working smart rather than working hard. That is why it allows the potential candidates to come along and accept the opportunity.

_Taking Care of the employees_ – Not only in terms of salary but also Amazon emphasizes the betterment of its workforce. Due to the ongoing business situation during the covid 19 pandemic, the company is trying to ensure the health of the workers in order to create a healthy work environment. It pays general attention to the safety of its employees by creating flexibility in the workplace. It also keeps encouraging the employees by giving them performance bonuses. The company is also offering 100 dollars to employees who can show proof of covid 19 vaccination, which is a unique way to compel the employees into doing something that can benefit both the company and the employees together.

_Embracing the updated mindset_ – Amazon, unlike other companies, understands the requirements of the generation. The number of employees Amazon has as of now is itself a huge number. Managing an enormous group of employees speaks for the management of the company. Certain employees of the company who don’t get the signing bonuses also get well compensated according to their workload and annual performance.

_With Strings Attached_ – While this all sounds wonderful, certain rules come along with the payments. There are a definite number of terms that the employee has to adhere to. In some cases, repayments to the company have been even more painful due to the hefty amount. The company has regulations on how long the employee should serve in order to keep the entire bonus without paying the company back anything. Also, not everyone is eligible for the signing bonus benefits. Even if Amazon has deep pockets, it has set standards for the employees when it comes to such benefits.

Working for Amazon is kind of a big deal nowadays due to its glowing reputation and tantalizing pay structures. It is good for the employee’s resume, the training that they receive and, of course, the perks along with the salary which can be overwhelming. But there is always a hidden side to every glowing face. It does offer a great signing bonus and special treatments to the employees but there is baggage that comes with it as well. Since Amazon has become a prime shopping hub for the global population due to the Covid 19 pandemic, no one prefers visiting the stores due to the virus contamination. But the workers don’t really have that option. Due to extreme workload, warehouse workers and delivery personnel are still out there working in order to get paid. Sometimes due to overstaffing, maintaining social distance can be challenging at the workplace. It can cause delays and hamper daily productivity. But now that the situation regarding the global health crisis is changing let us all hope that the livelihoods of the general population can improve and provide positive results.
