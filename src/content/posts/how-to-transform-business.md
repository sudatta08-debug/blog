## Strategic management

Business transformation starts with distinguishing between scaled organizational improvements and complete changes. In order to achieve a transformative solution for business growth, one can approach creating smaller projects and plotting them onto a transformation road map. Also sometimes traditional approaches to change management might solve the problem in the first place. It can provide answers to implement and execute the strategies. In the IT sector, the emphasis is not only to maintain the technical paradigm but also to prevent future possible obstructions. With digital transformation, products and business models in today’s competitive environment are increasingly being transformed by technology.

## Six areas to change

### Business model

Changes in the key elements of the business model canvas should be a top priority while transforming a business which can lead to strengthening the foundation of the organization. Guidelines for operations should be deployed skilfully.

### Know Your Competition

The organization should decide the competitive strategies by differentiating the products by competitor analysis and micro-environmental scan. It must be decided how to position the organization by being a cost leader in the market.

### Cost Management

There must be an explicit strategy present for cost-cutting and budgeting. Implementing new systems can be over the odds. So the finance team should be perceptive of the comprehensive changes.

### Organizational Changes

There should be a benchmark for the management to shape a disciplined and more controlled environment. Leaders should possess an eye for detail for the projects they have been assigned to.

### Technological Changes

Balancing the 20 years of reputation with new system transformation is extremely challenging. Strategy for the impeccable transformation of data storage, e-commerce, networking, data security, digitization, and web services should be aligned.

### Advertisement

The goal of customer satisfaction should be handled with delicacy. Customers should be well informed and must not be handled with exaggeration. Reaching out to target audiences (such as CTO, CIO, Mid Biz Owner, and IT Decision Maker) is absolutely necessary. Addressing customers is as important as product development.
