[Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria), a citadel of ancient civilization, is also a motherland of indigenous culture, from metalworking to tribal therapies. Whether people travel for business or leisure they always end up scrolling their phones in search of a star-rated menu. Often people ramble around Europe to the United States when it comes to exploring cuisines while African flavours are left disguised. The colloquialism of the [Yoruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoruba_people) population in the country will definitely surprise you with their untold timeless recipes as well as their dominating Lebanese-inspired menus.

### Suya

If you are a passionate foodie, this particular dish will be exceptionally anchored to your mind. The unique feature of the dish is the coated powdered spices of nuts coupled with garlic sauce and pickles as sides. The suya in all its folded and grilled completion resists easy definition and invites an eloquent look at the community that further widens the common perception of what it means to be enjoying – the Western.

### Jollof Rice

This can be considered the most consumed dish in the country. Formulated with several specific native ingredients, the custom blend of rice and optional meat taste one of a kind. Cooked in flavorful tomato puree, this rice will make you feel local if you are craving an authentic West-African spirit.

### Lebanese Shawarma and Hummus

Among all of the places that the country offers, immigrants mostly prefer middle eastern cuisine for, of course, its healthy options. Even if they all are platters of beautiful delights, some have become favorites because they are reliably loved again and again. In this case, the famous [shawarma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawarma) with olive oil dressed hummus is the winner. Specifically, the stuffed grilled meat mixed with its signature red and green spices and the side hummus i.e, chickpeas and garlic infused in virgin handmade olive oil is a combination that no one can say no to.

### Coffee

Well, an African country and a cup of steaming coffee, both go hand in hand. As the entrepreneurs are elevating the cafe culture in the country it is also one of the most traded commodities in the world. Being a tropical plant, coffee culture dwells in the tropical country comfortably. So trying a well-brewed cup of coffee is a must in this country.

### Chapman

One of the most loved mocktails of all time in Nigeria is Chapman. It is basically a nonalcoholic drink blended with several fruit syrups with cut fruits. On top of it, the use of freshly plucked mint leaves makes it the perfect tropical drink that one can ask for after having a spicy meal.

### Bakery Goods

And it is time for desert now. A part of the Nigerian economy is braced by agriculture, and the production of cocoa can’t be ignored. From textured cakes to extravagant wedding cakes, anything available is a party in the mouth itself. Even if cakes and ice-creams are not healthy options, people who have certain likes and dislikes will always obtain these kinds of deserts. But of course, the culinary scene of the country is evolving day by day and adapting the organic bakery profiles. Nevertheless, not only Nigerians but immigrants also have soft corners for their soft-baked loaves of bread. And the bakeries of the country definitely satiate the desires of the countless hearts with their versatility.
