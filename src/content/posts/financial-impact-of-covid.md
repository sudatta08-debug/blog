The Covid 19 virus, which was first identified in China, has become one of the deadliest types of viruses that can affect humans. It has infected over 160 countries and killed over 10 million people. The World Health Organization (WHO) declared the coronavirus pandemic, which has affected everyone from individuals to industry.

Due to the outbreak, many businesses have had a hard time running their operations. Many of them have temporarily or permanently closed their doors. The financial fragility of small businesses has caused many to close their doors temporarily.

The sudden increase in demand and the sudden change in health and safety measures have caused the negative effects of the pandemic to quickly rise. The survival of MSMEs was a real test. As a result, many small businesses have had a hard time surviving. They have to adapt to the new normal by using new sales techniques and digital technologies.

### Cash Flow Management
Analyzing and preparing the [cashflows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_flow) are the most important parts of a company. Getting the proper financial information is very important for a company. Unfortunately, the economic situation during the pandemic has made it difficult for small business owners to meet their daily financial obligations. This is why it is important that they take the necessary steps to address this issue.

Due to the decrease in revenue and the reduction in client payments, the operations of a company have become more critical. This has caused many organizations to rethink their strategy and adapt to survive. One of the most challenging factors that affected the company was the implementation of employee layoffs.

Managing the emergency recovery fund has become a terrifying situation for organizations. One of the most effective ways to manage this issue is by cutting down on unnecessary expenses. Doing so will allow the company to maintain its cash flow and avoid running out of money.

Although some expenses can be eliminated, such as insurance and rents, changes in these costs are likely to be made in order to mitigate the impact of the pandemic on the company's cash flow. It is important that the entire team is focused on improving the company's financial performance.

The product supply chain is a crucial part of any business. Due to the Covid crisis, the travel and hospitality sectors have been severely affected. This has affected the entire supply chain. The only solution is to purchase bulk products.

To minimize the impact of the crisis on the business, it is important that the organization adopt a digital strategy. This can help ease the process and keep the customers engaged. In addition, it can help small retailers and importers maintain their operations.

Due to the increasing number of factors that can affect the business, such as the lack of credit facilities and market reach, it is important that the organization work remotely to keep the business running. Having a clear understanding of the financial risks and a strong supply chain framework is also important to keep the company running smoothly.

Due to the limited resources that small businesses have, they are unable to flourish during times of crisis. Even if the government provides financial assistance, these types of situations can still affect the operations of a company. Being positive can help the organization bounce back from these types of situations.

### Employee Health Risks

Due to social distancing regulations, the employees had to stay at home and work remotely. Working remotely somehow has its pros and cons. Remote working may be a flexible approach for employees, but it is not as productive as working in the office environment. In small businesses, the funding is already limited for employee health insurance.

So keeping employee health concerns is also a vital part of organizations. Laying off employees is a complex but forced decision that companies have to make. It can drive the mental illness of employees. As important as physical health, mental health is also crucial for good productivity outcomes.

Loss of life can be painful emotionally for everyone. During these kinds of emotional and mental disturbances, companies must provide their cooperation for healthy relationships with the employees. Employees should feel positive about their job security in the company. Corporate management of small business organizations must intact the mental support of the employees.

Research finds that due to the pandemic, employees find themselves less productive. Gestures like small rewards and positive feedback can boost the productivity of employees. That is why strategic employee management should be on point to track daily productivity. Incorporating tools for office conversations can make employees feel the office vibes.

Several other ways to keep employees interested more in remote environments are flexible work timings, professional counselling, increasing paid leaves, setting employee goals, and arranging social events. Also, employee illness is unfortunate, and it hampers business operations which ultimately is a challenge to the company's financials.

Due to the limited number of clients, many small businesses are more pessimistic about their employees' job security. Being able to remain calm and constructive can help the employees get through these difficult times.

Due to the various factors that have affected small businesses' capital expenditures in the past year, they have decreased their planned expenditures for FY 2021.

This spending is expected to drive the demand for various sectors and increase the supply chain efficiency. One of the most important factors that have helped the recovery in demand is the increase in consumer spending on health care. This has led to a bounce-back in the demand for various products and services. E-commerce businesses have also been able to mitigate the effects of travel restrictions.
