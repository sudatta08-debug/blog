## Writing a post

Go to **/admin**, sign in with GitHub and choose **Posts → New post**. Write in the editor, add a cover image and a few topics, then press **Publish**. The site rebuilds itself and the post goes live in about a minute.

## The SEO fields

Every post has an **SEO** section:

- **Focus keyword**: the phrase you want to rank for, like "how to file ITR online".
- **Search title**: what Google shows as the blue link. Leave empty to use the post title.
- **Meta description**: the grey text under the link. 120–160 characters.
- **Canonical URL**: only fill this if the post was first published on another site.
- **Hide from search engines**: for posts you don't want on Google.

## Checking your SEO

Open **/admin/seo/** on your live site for a report card on every post, with a score and a list of fixes.
